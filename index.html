You start with E, A and T and build words from them. A new character only unlocks once every unlocked one is at 90% accuracy over its last 10 answers (at least 5). In Receive, none of each letter's last 5 answers may have needed the word played again. Numbers works the same way, starting with 5, 0 and 1. Once all 26 letters are unlocked, Sentences gives you everyday sentences, a few pangrams and some radio-contact phrases (plus sentences with numbers once all ten digits are unlocked), chosen to lean on the letters you find hardest. A new letter unlocks once the ones you are learning have settled. Each letter is scheduled on its own: it comes back after a gap that grows every time you get it right quickly, and shrinks to a few seconds when you miss it, so you spend your time on the letters you don't know yet. Timing follows the PARIS standard: a dit is one unit, a dah three, with one unit between elements. At 20 WPM a unit is 60 ms. Progress is saved in this browser.
